# 04 — User Personas

## Student

Needs simple profile completion, job discovery, clear match evidence, skill-gap context, and a realistic improvement plan. The main question is: “How prepared am I, and what should I do next?” Avoid language that treats a score as a final decision.

## Placement Officer

Needs fast filtering, eligibility clarity, ranking explanation, evidence comparison, low-confidence queues, and audited manual overrides. The main question is: “What needs my attention, and why?”

## Recruiter — future role

Would need scoped job creation and candidate visibility. Do not expose institution-wide student records by default. This role remains a design placeholder until its legal, product, and authorization scope is approved.

## System Administrator — future role

Would manage configuration and audits but must not automatically receive access to resumes or matching evidence. This role remains deferred.

## Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] Each persona has least-privilege data visibility and a focused dashboard.
