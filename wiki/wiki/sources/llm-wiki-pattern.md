# LLM Wiki Pattern — Source Note

## Provenance

This note summarizes the immutable [raw source](../../raw/2026-08-15-llm-wiki-pattern.md), provided on 2026-08-15.

## Key claims

- A maintained wiki can compound knowledge more effectively than query-time synthesis over raw documents alone.
- LLMs can handle cross-references, summaries, updates, and bookkeeping while humans curate sources and steer questions.
- Indexing, chronological logging, and periodic linting give the system durable navigation and maintenance discipline.
- Obsidian and Git are suitable interfaces/storage for a Markdown-based implementation.

## Interpretation

**Inference:** This approach is most reliable when updates preserve provenance and explicitly identify uncertainty. Without those controls, it can turn into a polished but untraceable collection of summaries.
