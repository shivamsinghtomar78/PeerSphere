# Shivam Singh Knowledge Wiki

This is an Obsidian-compatible, LLM-maintained knowledge wiki. Open this folder as a vault in Obsidian and begin at [[wiki/index]].

- Add untouched source material to `raw/`.
- Ask the LLM to **ingest** a specific source; it will update the wiki, index, and log.
- Ask the LLM to **query** the wiki for cited synthesis.
- Ask the LLM to **lint** the wiki periodically for stale, disconnected, or contradictory notes.

The initial source and its compiled notes demonstrate the pattern. The governing workflow is in [AGENTS.md](AGENTS.md).
